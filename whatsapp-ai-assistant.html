WhatsApp AI Assistant

Give your customers instant answers on WhatsApp, day and night. Local Boost Lab sets up an AI assistant, supervised by our team, that replies to your customers, books appointments and sends reminders, so you never miss a lead or a booking.

Request a demo → How it works
What it does

Your customers get an answer. You get the booking.

  • Answers common questions such as address, prices, hours and services.
  • Schedules appointments and confirms them.
  • Sends appointment reminders so fewer people miss their visit.
  • Hands off to your team when a conversation needs a person.
  • Works on your own WhatsApp Business number, and every conversation stays private to your business.
