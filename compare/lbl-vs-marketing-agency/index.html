Which one fits you

When an agency is the better choice
  • You also need a website, ads, social media or branding, and you want one team to handle them together.
  • You want a custom strategy and campaigns, not a steady weekly routine.
  • You want a named person you can call and meet with.
  • Your business needs marketing across several locations or channels at once.
When Local Boost Lab is the better fit
  • Your main need is a Google profile that stays active, with reviews answered quickly.
  • You want a predictable monthly cost and no contract.
  • You prefer email to meetings and calls.
  • You already have other marketing in place and only need the profile handled.

How to decide in three questions

How to decide in three questions
  1. Is the Google Business Profile the main thing you need handled, or one piece of a bigger marketing plan?
  2. Do you want a steady routine (replies, posts, a report) or a custom strategy?
  3. Do you need to talk to someone, or is email enough?
What to ask any agency before you sign
  • What exactly is included, in writing, and what is billed extra?
  • Who answers reviews, and how fast? What happens with a negative review?
  • What is the minimum term, and what happens if you cancel?
  • Will you keep ownership of your Google profile, with the agency only getting access?
  • What will the monthly report show?
