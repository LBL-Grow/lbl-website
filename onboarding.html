🔍 How to find your Google Business Profile URL

  1. Go to Google Maps and search for your business name.
  2. Click on your business in the results.
  3. Click "Share" → then "Copy link".
  4. Paste that link here. It usually starts with https://maps.google.com or https://g.page/
